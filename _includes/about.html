About

Homegrown in Steamboat Springs. No jargon. No outsourcing.

  • 2020-2023

    Our Humble Beginnings

    When COVID shut down Steamboat, Ayric Ingram saw an opportunity: a tight-knit mountain community that deserved local, trustworthy cybersecurity. The next three years were spent earning certifications, building skills, and laying the groundwork.

  • March 2023

    An Agency is Born

    Shjen Securities opened its doors — built from the ground up in Steamboat Springs, for Steamboat Springs. Local clients. Real protection. No jargon.

  • December 2025

    Transition to Full Service

    What started as password management grew into a full-service operation: network hardening, IT infrastructure, and ongoing security support. Today Shjen Securities works with homeowners and small businesses across Steamboat Springs — protecting the people who have the most to lose.
