Do I really need cybersecurity as a small business or home user?

Yes — small businesses and home users are among the most targeted, precisely because they're assumed to have weak defenses. Attackers don't always go after big corporations; they go after easy targets. A single compromised account, network breach, or ransomware infection can cost far more to recover from than proactive protection ever would.

What does network hardening actually mean?

Network hardening means reducing the number of ways an attacker can get into your network. In practice, this includes setting up a proper firewall, segmenting your devices with VLANs (so your smart TV can't talk to your work laptop), securing your Wi-Fi with strong encryption, and closing unnecessary access points. Think of it as locking the windows and doors — and making sure the locks actually work.

How do I know if I've already been compromised?

Common warning signs include: slow or unusual device behavior, unexpected password reset emails, accounts you don't recognize, unknown devices on your network, or being locked out of your own accounts. That said, many compromises go undetected for months. If you're unsure, a security assessment is the fastest way to find out — that's exactly what our free consultation covers.
