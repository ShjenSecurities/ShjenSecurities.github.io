I'm back in Tokyo after having my fun in the Southwestern part of Japan. I'm not usually much of a big city type, but Tokyo is full of wonderment! So many things to do & see. Incredibly different than a tiny town like Steamboat Springs, CO. After checking into my apartment, I wandered upon an extravagant garden — Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden. It had two main sections, one was a traditional Japanese garden & the other was a cultivated rose garden. There were over 50 varieties of unique rose bushes, each rose more beautiful & different in color than the next — ranging from pure white to dark purple. I've never seen anything like it! Meanwhile, the traditional section was composed of water, rock, shrubbery & tree features. Deeply beautiful.

Once I left the park, I wandered into a random ramen shop that caught my eye — Ichikakuya Ramen. It had a bright neon sign showing a bowl, nori, chashu, negi, & a soft-boiled egg. Ichikakuya had my favorite type of ramen, featuring a tonkotsu broth, marinated naruto & black garlic oil. The restaurant was quite narrow, with plenty of bar seats. I squeezed in, handed my ticket order to the chef at the bar. Within five minutes the steaming bowl arrived. I was happily enjoying the aromatic symphony composed from blending chashu, charred garlic, & nori. Without hesitation I slurped away!
