It is getting close to New Year's — apparently everything closes for most of the week, plus the Eve. That being said, today was my final day to experience good ramen & to do any last minute sight-seeing before my departure on the second. I figured I might as well go out with a bang & see the famous Tokyo Tower & see the views from its fabled top deck. I was not disappointed! The mid-deck was really crowded, but also had nice views of every section of the city, including a cool light show displaying on the windows creating a snowing effect. I wandered this area about an hour before being admitted to the top deck. This section rises 819 ft and offers spectacular views of every area of Tokyo. It is impossible to describe the breath-taking metropolis from this vantage. The size & sprawl of Tokyo is truly astounding and a testament to not only the country's legendary engineering capabilities, but also its resiliency — Tokyo has been destroyed several times by natural disasters & completely rebuilt each time during its history. In an effort to truly understand the height of the tower, I opted to take the 600+ stairs down from the mid-deck to the walking plaza, a journey that took about 10 minutes of treading down staircases!

As one can imagine, that kind of exercise builds quite the appetite. I had ramen on my mind and happened to luckily walk past a shop with about 20 people waiting outside for their turn. Well, I became #21 in line without hesitation, nor knowledge of just how lucky my feet were. After about a 45 minute wait I was shown inside. I ordered the Special Shoyu Ramen & waited eagerly. This place was so clean & efficient. Every stainless surface was polished to a mirror finish — the upkeep must have taken hours every night to maintain, it was incredibly impressive. It was then I noticed the plaques denoting its status as a Michelin star rated restaurant — the highest honor a restaurant can achieve! I could see how they achieved this illustrious status. I watched the chef "comb" the noodles before folding the ends underneath, exposing an arch of noodles, each strand perfectly in-line. The chashu pork a beautiful pink hue & soft as could be — melting in your mouth. The egg a perfect soft boil. Fresh made wontons, their dough impossibly thin & flavorful. To say the broth was undeniably the most delicately complex & delicious broth I've ever tasted, would be an understatement. It was so pure & clean. The umami & shoyu flavors joining together to create a flavor profile that my developed palate had never experienced — & may never again! All of this was complimented perfectly with a crystal clear phat, perfectly coating the bouncy noodles. Lastly, the contents were topped with a brilliant green sprig of micro chives, lending a refreshing taste to every bite.
