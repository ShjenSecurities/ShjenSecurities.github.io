New section of Tokyo, new place to explore away from the crazy bustle of Shinjuku. It is so peaceful here, little parks & shrines intermittently placed. The maples explode with color — golden yellow, burnt orange, burgundy — perfectly paired with the sun poking through the foliage, enhancing the natural colors well.

Lunch time has come, thus more ramen to experience! I found an interesting place. The wooden sign displaying a kanji within a ramen bowl. A little stand holds two wooden blocks "OPEN" on one & "YUJI Ramen" on the next stacked on top of each other. Inside, the space is small & cozy with six bar seats & a few tables. I sat front & center at the bar so I could watch the single chef work his culinary magic. The menu is diverse & every item looks delicious. The chef explains their process of using tuna bones as the base for the tonkotsu broth, something I've never heard of. I was easily convinced to order the chef's recommendation — tonkotsu ramen with dumplings. The dumplings consisted of green curry, house & special. The crust highlights the potent fresh dough seared in the pan, creating an incredible golden crust connecting them all. Meanwhile the ramen were delightful, featuring a house-made kimchi — spicy, fresh, fermented, fishy, with a porky punch. This is what I came to Japan to experience!
